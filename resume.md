# Aizen Sosuke
### Security Researcher & Bug Bounty Hunter

Independent security researcher focused on **web application security** and
**responsible disclosure**. I identify high-impact vulnerabilities in authorized
bug bounty programs and report them responsibly and clearly.

---

## 🎯 Areas of Expertise

- **Server-Side Request Forgery (SSRF)** — cloud metadata, DNS rebinding, filter/redirect bypasses
- **GraphQL Security** — authorization flaws, introspection, batching abuse
- **IDOR / BOLA** — broken object-level authorization
- **Access Control & Privilege Escalation** — horizontal and vertical
- **Business Logic Flaws** — workflow, pricing, and validation gaps
- **Authentication** — JWT attacks, OAuth pitfalls
- **Client-Side** — XSS (reflected, stored, DOM), CORS, race conditions

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies

## 🔬 Approach & Ethics

All testing is performed **strictly within the authorized scope** of official bug
bounty programs, following each program's rules of engagement and responsible
disclosure practices. My focus is finding genuine high-impact issues, demonstrating
them safely without touching real user data, and writing reports a triage team can
act on quickly.
