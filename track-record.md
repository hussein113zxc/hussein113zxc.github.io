## Focus areas

- **GraphQL** — authorization bypass, batching abuse, introspection exposure
- **SSRF** — cloud metadata access, SSRF-to-cloud credential theft
- **IDOR / BOLA** — broken object-level authorization
- **Authentication** — JWT and OAuth flaws
- **Access control** — horizontal & vertical privilege escalation
- **Business logic** — workflow and price-manipulation flaws
