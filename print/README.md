Final printed size: **1600 × 2000 mm** (1.6 m wide × 2 m tall), portrait.

All raster files carry embedded resolution metadata, so they open at the true
physical size (1.60 m × 2.00 m) rather than being misread as 72 dpi.

Prefer the **vector PDF** — it cannot pixelate at any size. The raster files are
provided for print workflows that require a bitmap.

Artwork is full-bleed: the background reaches every edge, so a few millimetres
of trim will not expose a white border.
